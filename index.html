About

waxfreq* is Gioia Ceravolo and Simon “sib3n” Gromic, an independent producer and DJ duo out of Basel, driven by a shared obsession with hardware, raw sounds and vinyl culture.

Everything starts on machines. Tracks are built hands-on, cut straight to wax and played out in vinyl-only sets, their own productions sitting next to carefully selected records. Somewhere between techno and minimal, hypnotic and Detroit-leaning, always on the deeper end of the floor.

Beyond music, both work as visual artists. Their practice moves from oil painting to sculpture, mixed media and sound performance, with the same hands-on approach that shapes the records.
